### **Assignment 4 – Mini Brand Guide**

**Purpose / Objectives**

* Develop visual consistency and identity for the app  
* Introduce basic branding concepts: typography, color, logo

**Description**  
Complete the work you had been doing in class: 

* Create a new page in your Figma project group titled “Last Name \- Brand Guide”. Copy and paste the [brand guide template](https://www.figma.com/design/sKgFr1Zs6TVxSfKCrX3sZ3/Style-Guide-Template?node-id=0-1) into your page. Your mini brand guide will include:  
  * 1-2 primary colors  
  * 4 Accent Colors  
  * 4 gray or neutral colors  
  * H1 \- H5 fonts  
  * Body and inline link fonts  
  * A primary, secondary button, and inline button

  *Optional* 

  * *Iconography \- [Google Material Icons](https://fonts.google.com/icons) \- You can customize the icons to fit your style.*  
  * *Wordmark or logo*  
  * *Notes on tone or personality (1–2 bullet points)*

**Deliverables and Submission Instructions**

* In your GROUP Project Repository, create a branch \- LASTNAME-brandguide  
* On your branch, in a folder named LASTNAME-brandguide. Create a [README.md](http://README.md) file containing:  
  * Link to your Figma file (**shared link**) containing your brand guide.  
  * Add screenshots / embed exports of your brand guide

* The UI UX mentors will help your team select a “winning” brand guide that best follows good practice.
